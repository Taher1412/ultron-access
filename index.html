ultron access

A private, single-user integration. Not offered to the public.

ultron access lets Taher Ayadi's personal assistant, Phil, read his own Gmail and Google Calendar so it can summarize new mail and upcoming events for him. It runs on his private server and only ever connects to his own Google account.

Access is read-only: the app cannot send, delete or change email, and cannot create or edit calendar events.

Privacy policy

ultron access · last updated 22 October 2026

ultron access is a private tool built and used by one person, Taher Ayadi, to give his personal assistant ("Phil") read access to his own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

Only for the Google account that authorizes it, and only read-only:

  • Gmail messages and settings (gmail.readonly)
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.readonly)
  • The account's email address, to identify which account is connected

It cannot send, delete or modify email, and cannot create, change or delete calendar events.

How the data is used

Mail and calendar data is read on demand to answer the account owner's own requests, for example a morning summary of new mail and today's events. To write those answers, the relevant excerpts are processed by the AI model provider the assistant uses. Data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used by this app to train AI models.

Storage

The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Mail and calendar content is not copied into a separate database; it is fetched when needed.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone other than the AI model provider mentioned above, and only to produce responses for the account owner.

ultron access's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
